SCOPE design notes

SCOPE demo

A payer pays two people on Solana devnet. The amounts are encrypted, and one recipient gets a fresh account that the public cannot tie to him. He collects the money without holding SOL, then proves that one payment to an auditor. Two minutes, captioned.

Every transaction in the video ran on devnet on 13 October 2026. On-chain waits are shortened. Check them yourself:
