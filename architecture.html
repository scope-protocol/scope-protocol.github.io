SCOPE

A company pays many people on Solana. Amounts are encrypted, and a stealth recipient gets a fresh account per payment that nobody else can tie to them. The company stays visible, and an auditor can still read amounts.

1 · Overview

What is SCOPE?

A payout rail where the payer is public and the payments are not.

HiddenEvery amount

Transfers are Token-2022 confidential transfers: the ledger holds ciphertext.

HiddenWho a stealth recipient is

Each payment lands in a fresh account that only the recipient's scan key recognizes.

Visible on purposeThe payer

No pool, no relayer, no mixer. Amounts stay recoverable by receipt or the mint's auditor key.

Not claimed: privacy from the payer, privacy for the payer, anonymity. Merging several payments into one account links them again.

3 · System

What runs where, and what touches money?

Two command-line clients, Solana's own token programs, and one small program of ours beside the money path.

The payer sets up a stranger's account

Token-2022 checks only the current owner's signature. The payer owns the account while it configures it, then hands it to the recipient's one-time key P.

Nobody can squat the address

It is create_with_seed(payer, R), which only the payer can allocate. A key shared by both sides was considered and not taken.

Exactly one payment lands

announce refuses the account unless it takes one credit at most and no public credits.

4 · One payment

What happens to the one-time account?

Seven states across two atomic transactions. The chain itself is the status.

Every instruction, and what our program checks

Payer transaction hover to find the state

  1. System CreateAccountWithSeed
  2. InitializeAccount3, owner = payer
  3. ConfigureAccount, credit cap 1
  4. VerifyPubkeyValidity
  5. DisableNonConfidentialCredits
  6. SetAuthority CloseAccount → PDA
  7. SetAuthority AccountOwner → P
  8. announce
  9. System Transfer payer → P, lamports
  10. Transfer payer → account
  11. three proof verifications

Recipient transaction

  1. Create and configure D, P pays
  2. ApplyPendingBalance
  3. Transfer whole balance → D, three proofs
  4. EmptyAccount
  5. Burn public dust, only if nonzero
  6. reclaim
  7. System Transfer, P's remainder → D_owner

announce accepts the account only if

  • its address is create_with_seed(payer, R)
  • its close authority is the announcement PDA
  • the payer handed it over and left no delegate
  • it takes one confidential credit and no public ones

reclaim needs no signer, and

  • finds the announcement at ["ann", account] with its stored bump, so a decoy account cannot match
  • refunds only the stored payer
  • leaves the closing to Token-2022, which refuses while any balance remains
