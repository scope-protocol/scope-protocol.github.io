Overview

How does one payment travel?

One transaction from the payer, one from the recipient, and a one-time account in between that the payer opens and the recipient empties.

hidden

Every amount

All rows are Token-2022 confidential transfers. The ledger holds ElGamal ciphertexts and zero-knowledge proofs, never a plain number.

hidden

Who a stealth recipient is

Each payment lands in a fresh account owned by a fresh key. Only the recipient's scan key recognises it.

visible on purpose

The payer

No pool, no relayer, no mixer. The payer signs every payment and can prove any amount to an auditor.

A payroll run

What does a run look like on chain?

Acme pays Alice, who has a known confidential account, and Bob, who uses SCOPE's one-time accounts. Step through the real devnet run and switch whose eyes you look through.

What runs where

Which parts touch money?

Two command-line clients, Solana's own token and proof programs, and one small program of ours beside the money path.

The payer sets up a stranger's account

Token-2022 checks only the current owner's signature. The payer owns the new account while it configures it, then hands it to the recipient's one-time key.

Nobody can squat the address

The one-time account lives at create_with_seed(payer, seed(R)), an address only the payer can allocate.

At most one payment lands

announce refuses the account unless it accepts one confidential credit at most and no public credits.

One payment

What happens to the one-time account?

Seven states across two atomic transactions. The chain itself is the status: an announcement that still exists is a payment not yet collected. Point at an instruction to see the state it moves the account into.

The 68 bytes

What does the program store?

One announcement per stealth payment, at the address ["ann", T]. These are the real bytes of Bob's announcement, read from devnet before he collected it.

Keys

Where does the privacy come from?

One shared secret per payment, split into independent keys. Colour shows who can compute each one.

Code

Where does each piece live?

One cargo workspace: one Pinocchio program, one Rust CLI, three libraries. The program and the client meet only in crates/interface.
