1. Project

SCOPE is a confidential payout rail on Solana. A visible payer pays many recipients with encrypted amounts, and a recipient who wants it gets a fresh one-time account per payment that the public cannot tie to them. Funds stay ordinary Token-2022 tokens in Token-2022 accounts: no pool, no relayer, no custody. Launch use: payroll and supplier payouts, where the payer is public anyway.

The on-chain part is one Pinocchio program with two instruction handlers, announce and reclaim. Moving tokens is done by Token-2022 instructions the clients compose; the program is never in the funds path. The off-chain part is one Rust CLI, scope, for the payer and the recipient.

Custom program constraints

  • An announced token account takes at most one confidential credit and no public credits (REQ10, REQ11).
  • Only the payer can create the token account address, and announce checks the address binds payer and R (REQ05).
  • The close authority of every announced account is its announcement PDA, so rent can only return to the stored payer (REQ07, REQ19, REQ20).
  • The program holds no authority that can move tokens; CloseAccount works only at zero balance (REQ20).
  • Error codes are Custom(n), append-only, 0 to 17. Compute: announce 6,373 CU at bump 255, reclaim 3,256 CU, with CI ceilings 7,700 and 4,000.

8. On-chain vs client-side

Protocol logic on chain is only what the two handlers check and write, plus Token-2022's own transfer rules. Everything else runs in the scope CLI and is labelled CL, not REQ, because no program of ours enforces it.
